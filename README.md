The SkyMonitor NWR Archive records EAS alerts issued on NOAA Weather Radio, similar to how CAR (Central Alert Repository) records EAS alerts for TV or other sources. Unlike CAR, the SkyMonitor NWR Archive records EAS alerts on publicly available online NWR audio streams, and you can also view and listen to the streams that are captured by SkyMonitor NWR Archive directly in our website, at https://nwr.skymonitor.app/nwr.
